1. Screen recording
The attached video was recorded on an iPhone running the latest iOS. It starts at app launch and shows the typical flow: the tutorial (buy the first business, collect coins, upgrade, hire a helper), switching maps, the Store (only in-game gems; no real-money purchases in this version), missions and daily reward, the leaderboard with the report/hide options on another player's name, the Profile with the one-time name change, and the account deletion flow (Profile > Excluir conta).

2. Purpose and audience
Império de Bolso ("Pocket Empire") is a casual idle tycoon game for teens and adults (13+). The player starts with a water cart on the beach and grows a business empire across 8 maps by collecting coins, upgrading buildings and hiring helpers. It offers short, relaxing play sessions, a sense of progression and friendly weekly competition.

3. How to access the main features
No login or credentials are needed. On first launch the app creates a guest account automatically. A short tutorial guides the first steps; the bottom bar gives access to Build, Helpers, Store, Map and Profile, and the side buttons open Missions, Ranking, Daily reward and the weekend Event. Language can be changed in Settings (Portuguese, English, Spanish).

4. External services
Our own backend (api-imperio.crowntech.com.br, hosted by Crown Tech LTDA) stores progress, the in-game economy and the leaderboard. No third-party analytics, ads, payment processors, authentication providers or AI services are used in this version.

5. Regional differences
The app works the same in every region where it is available. It is currently not distributed in the European Union.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material
Not applicable. The game contains no real-money gambling or financial services. Artwork was created for the game by Crown Tech LTDA (with AI-assisted generation); music and sound effects are CC0 (public domain) assets.

User-generated content
The only user-generated content is the player's display name (changeable once). Names are validated on the server against a blocklist, any player can report an inappropriate name or hide a player from the leaderboard, and names reported by several players are automatically reset.

Account deletion
Profile > Excluir conta deletes the account and all its data immediately. It is also available on the web at https://imperio.crowntech.com.br/excluir-conta/.
